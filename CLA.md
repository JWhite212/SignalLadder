Thank you for contributing to SignalLadder. This agreement sets out the terms on which you give your contribution to the project. You keep the copyright in your work: this agreement is a licence, not a transfer.

## In short

This summary is here to help you read the agreement. It is not part of it, and where the two differ, the agreement below is what counts.

- **You keep the copyright** in what you contribute.
- **You give the maintainer a licence** to use, change and distribute your contribution under any terms, including terms other than the GPL. That is what lets signed builds be sold on other terms if that is ever needed.
- **You give a patent licence** for any patents of yours that your contribution uses.
- **You confirm that the work is yours to give**, and that your employer or client, if they could own it, has agreed.
- **Nothing obliges the project to use your contribution**, and you give it as it is, with no warranty.

## The agreement

### 1. Definitions

In this agreement:

- **"Maintainer"** means Jamie White, the owner of the SignalLadder project, and anyone to whom he transfers the project.
- **"You"** means the individual who agrees to this agreement.
- **"Project"** means the SignalLadder software and its documentation, as published at <https://github.com/JWhite212/SignalLadder> or any place the Maintainer moves it to.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including code, documentation, images and tests, that You Submit to the Project, and any changes You make to it.
- **"Submit"** means to send a Contribution to the Maintainer or the Project in any form, including by pull request, issue, comment or email, with the intention that it be included in the Project. Material You clearly mark as "not a contribution" is not Submitted.

This is a licence. You keep the copyright in Your Contributions, and You remain free to use them in any way You wish.

If anyone starts patent litigation against You or anyone else, including a cross-claim or counterclaim, alleging that Your Contribution or the Project infringes a patent, any patent licence that party received under this agreement ends on the date that litigation is filed.

### 4. Moral rights

To the extent the law allows, You waive, and agree not to assert, any moral rights You have in Your Contributions against the Maintainer or anyone who receives the Project from the Maintainer, so that they can change and distribute Your Contributions as this agreement allows. ### 5. What You confirm

You confirm that:

1. Each Contribution is Your original work, or You have the right to Submit it under this agreement.
2. You are legally entitled to grant the licences in this agreement.
3. If an employer, client or anyone else has, or may have, rights in what You create, including intellectual property You create for them, You have their permission to Submit Your Contributions under this agreement, or they have given up those rights in Your Contributions.

5. You will tell the Maintainer if You learn that anything You confirmed in this clause is no longer true.

Unless You agree otherwise in writing, or the law requires it, You provide Your Contributions "as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind, including warranties of title, non-infringement, merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose. You do not have to provide support for Your Contributions, though You may choose to.

### 7. Contributions made before this agreement

This agreement also covers any Contribution You Submitted to the Project before You agreed to it.

### 8. Changes to this agreement

If the Maintainer publishes a new version of this agreement, it applies only to Contributions You Submit after You agree to that new version. Contributions You have already Submitted stay under the version You agreed to.

This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute about it.

## How to agree

When You open Your first pull request, CLA Assistant will comment on it with a link. Follow the link, sign in with GitHub and accept this agreement, and CLA Assistant records that You have. You only need to do this once. Your agreement then covers everything You Submit to the Project, unless You tell the Maintainer otherwise in writing.
