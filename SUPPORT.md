# Getting help

SignalLadder is a free, pre-release project with one maintainer. There is no support contract and no promised response time, but every report is read, and a missed alert is always treated as the most serious kind of problem.

## Look here first

Most questions are answered by the menu, the Inspector and these guides:

- **[Getting started](docs/getting-started.md)**: building the app, granting permissions and making your first rule.
- **[Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md)**: what the health line means, and what to do when nothing is captured, no sound plays or an alert sounds twice.
- **[Writing rules by hand](docs/rules-format.md)**: every key, operator, alert and error message in the rules file.
- **[Privacy](docs/privacy.md)**: what SignalLadder reads, what it keeps and how to remove it all.

## Where to go next

| You have | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Something that is broken, or an alert that did not sound | A [bug report](https://github.com/JWhite212/SignalLadder/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). [Collecting information for a bug report](docs/troubleshooting.md#collecting-information-for-a-bug-report) lists what helps. |
| An idea for a feature | A [feature request](https://github.com/JWhite212/SignalLadder/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Check the [roadmap](README.md#roadmap) first. |
| A question the guides do not answer | A [feature request](https://github.com/JWhite212/SignalLadder/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) is fine: say it is a question, and the answer will usually end up in the docs. |
| A security problem | Not a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). |

Before you paste anything into an issue, replace every name, channel and message with a placeholder. Notifications carry other people's words.
